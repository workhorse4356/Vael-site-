A Space for
Contemporary Vision

Founded in 2008, Vael Galerie has established itself as one of Europe's foremost platforms for contemporary and digital art, located in the heart of Paris's Marais district.

Our programme is built on the belief that art has the power to transform how we see the world. Over seventeen years, we have placed work in major public and private collections across five continents, and organised exhibitions at Art Basel, FIAC, and Frieze.
